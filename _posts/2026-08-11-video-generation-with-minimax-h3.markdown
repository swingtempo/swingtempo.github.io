A few days ago I finally installed a 32GB AMD R9700 GPU, for AI coding and other projects.

Today the Minimax H3 video generation model came out, so I decided to check it out using ComfyUI for the first time.

Getting ComfyUI running on AMD is still not ideal, but there are good instructions here: <https://comfyui-amd.github.io/>.

I ran their sample template and prompt and generated a 5s clip, and it took under 9 minutes.
