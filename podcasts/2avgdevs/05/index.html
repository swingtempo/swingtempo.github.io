02The superchipNVIDIA RTX Spark N1X

One package, two dielets, one memory pool

RTX Spark is the consumer, Windows-facing version of the GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip that has powered Linux DGX Spark mini-PCs since late 2025 — ServeTheHome calls it “the consumer iteration of NVIDIA’s GB10 chip.”13 The launch silicon is the N1X, shipping in two configurations; Microsoft’s configurator labels both “NVIDIA RTX Spark™ N1X.”2

Selected regionTwo dielets, joined in 2.5D

Pick a block on the left. RTX Spark is not one chip in the usual sense: TechInsights’ teardown found two TSMC 3nm dielets — an “S-die” carrying the CPU and memory subsystem and a “G-die” carrying the Blackwell GPU — joined in 2.5D packaging using Amkor S-SWIFT fan-out, a first for a commercial product.17 Everything below sits inside that one package.

Both laptop columns are labelled “RTX Spark N1X” by NVIDIA.2 The 18-core part’s memory ceiling is a live source disagreement: NVIDIA’s spec table says 64GB, The Verge reported 32GB, and Surface’s own configurator ships it only with 24GB or 32GB.

The honest constraint is bandwidth

  • Interconnect. NVLink-C2C between CPU and GPU at about 600 GB/s — 5× PCIe Gen 5 bandwidth.14
  • Memory. Up to 128GB LPDDR5X on a 256-bit interface, 273 GB/s bandwidth (GB10 spec). This is the honest constraint: every independent benchmark shows LLM generation speed is bandwidth-bound.515
  • “1 petaflop.” Theoretical FP4 with sparsity, per Microsoft’s own footnote. Hot Chips 2025 slides list about 31 TFLOPS FP32 and 1,000 TOPS NVFP4 for GB10.16
  • Packaging. Two TSMC 3nm dielets — “S-die” (CPU + memory subsystem) and “G-die” (Blackwell GPU) — joined in 2.5D. TechInsights identified Amkor S-SWIFT fan-out, a first for a commercial product.17
  • Co-design. MediaTek co-designed the CPU. SVP Vince Hu: “With RTX Spark, MediaTek and NVIDIA bring unprecedented local AI supercomputing directly to consumer PCs.”18

Claims, caveats, and the platform layer

Unsubstantiated efficiency claim

NVIDIA calls RTX Spark “the most power-efficient RTX chip ever made” — with, as The Verge noted, “not so much as a single statistic or chart to back that up.”19

  • Not shipping this year. A smaller N1 family (12-/10-core, 2,560/2,048 CUDA) exists in leaks but is not shipping in 2026.20
  • Microsoft wrote the platform layer. Workload Profile Scheduling for the 20-core CPU, its Power and Thermal Framework, Prism emulation tuning, and a higher, smarter GPU-accessible memory limit with larger page sizes on unified-memory systems.21

Term, once: unified memory means the CPU and GPU address the same physical pool instead of copying work across a PCIe bus — which is what lets a 128GB laptop hold a model that no consumer GPU of matching cost could fit in VRAM.

03Surface Laptop UltraMicrosoft’s tagline: “The new shape of power”

A MacBook Pro competitor that opens like a Surface

Microsoft’s positioning is blunt: the most powerful Surface Laptop ever, aimed at creators, developers and AI builders — a direct MacBook Pro competitor. Under the lid is the N1X; around it is the most heavily re-engineered chassis Microsoft has shipped.

Signature feature 01Magnetic Connect

“The world’s first built-in magnetic USB-C charging on a laptop.” Magnets surround a standard USB-C port on the right side; any USB-C cable works, and the port remains fully functional for video and data. The Verge’s behind-the-scenes counted roughly 300 iterations to get the pull strength right.24

Signature feature 02Built for sustained performance

A new thermal architecture: smaller motherboard, bigger fans, bigger battery, and an elevated base to feed airflow. Microsoft claims up to 2.5× the thermal capacity of the Surface Laptop 15-inch (8th Edition).24

Signature feature 03Repairable by design

No glued parts, QR-code repair guidance, internal wayfinding, and a removable M.2 SSD. Engadget notes user-replaceable storage that a reviewer swapped for a 2TB third-party drive.25

Signature feature 04Local AI headroom

Run 120B-parameter models locally. Microsoft event materials went further, showing “up to 284 billion parameter models run locally on RTX Spark laptops”; The Verge reported Microsoft claims compressed 280B models.2627

Signature feature 05Copilot+ capable, unlabeled

The chip includes an NPU meeting the 40-TOPS Copilot+ bar, though Microsoft has retired the “Copilot+ PC” label from Surface marketing. “These are not called Copilot+ PCs. They do meet all the requirements of our previous bar,” said Brett Ostrum, Surface CVP.28

7.2 — Local intelligence (the models and the runtime)

The runtime layer

7.4 — The strategic bet

Windows-on-Arm history

Surface RT ran NVIDIA Tegra. Surface Pro X used Qualcomm SQ chips. Copilot+ launched on Snapdragon X in 2024. RTX Spark is the first time this decade that a vendor besides Qualcomm supplies the SoC for a Windows box — “Microsoft has more riding on the Windows-on-Arm ecosystem than ever.”13

Who it is aimed at

ServeTheHome reads the Surface as “a fresh broadside at… Apple,” which has captured much of the developer market.13

Qualcomm isn’t standing still

8.2 — AI developers

CUDA Toolkit 13.4

Enables native Windows-on-Arm development, with ARM64EC and cross-compilation. NVIDIA also ships an RTX Spark porting guide and a porting hub.54

llama.cpp: 2× inference gains

OpenShell — NVIDIA’s open-source secure runtime for agents

Sandboxed execution; a supervisor that checks every outbound request against policy; credential management; YAML policies compiled to OPA/Rego; OCSF logging; and a formal policy prover that mathematically verifies permissions. On Windows it integrates with MXC. Hermes Agent (Nous Research) and OpenClaw adopt it; named enterprise users include Cadence, Slack and Gecko Robotics.55

Flagged honestly: NIM on Windows on Arm

NIM microservices exist in the DGX Spark ecosystem, but Windows-on-Arm NIM support is limited and unproven so far.56

What the gaming story is really for

Gaming is the trust signal for the harder technical claim: that an Arm Windows machine can run x86 game binaries through Prism emulation without spoiling frame times. The on-stage Gears of War: E-Day demo (§6) was the proof point Microsoft chose to build the whole event around.

8.4 — Scaling the ladder, and the roadmap

DGX Spark stays the pure CUDA appliance

The 64GB model at $4,999 clusters two units into 128GB / 200B-parameter capability over ConnectX-7 200GbE — a trick the Surface Dev Box cannot do, having no ConnectX-7.9

DGX Station for Windows, Q4 2026

The GB300 Grace Blackwell Ultra Desktop Superchip: 72-core Grace, up to 748GB coherent memory (LPDDR5X SOCAMM2 + HBM3e), 20 petaflops FP4, ConnectX-8 at 800Gb/s, and up to 1-trillion-parameter models (Llama 4 Maverick, Kimi K2.6, DeepSeek V4 Pro). NVIDIA positions it as a “Personal AI Supercomputer” and a “Token Factory” for 32+ simultaneous agents. First systems: Dell Pro Precision with GB300 and the HP ZGX Fury AI Station.1258

MediaTek, now strategic

NVIDIA’s $3.5B convertible-bond investment on August 31, 2026, with NVLink Fusion access, locks in the partner that co-designed the CPU.8

Roadmap: N2X and N3X — and R2-D2

Huang has confirmed both next generations are planned, with the endgame a voice-driven “Star Trek computer”: “I want to talk to my laptop! I want R2-D2!… Satya and I, we’re going to walk up to our Windows PC and go ‘hello, do something.’”59
